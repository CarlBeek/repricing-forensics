Glamsterdam ships two gas-schedule changes. Here's what each one breaks.

EIP-7904 and EIP-8037 are both scheduled for the next Ethereum hard fork (Glamsterdam, later this year). A modified reth node replays historical mainnet blocks under each proposed schedule independently, comparing baseline and repriced execution outcomes transaction by transaction.

EIP-7904 — Opcode repricing

Raises the gas cost of eight EVM opcodes (KECCAK256, DIV, MOD, EXP, and four siblings) whose prices have not been updated since Frontier. Most breakages occur in contracts that hardcode gas in inner CALLs or rely on the 2300 transfer stipend.

EIP-8037 — Native state gas

Introduces a static cost-per-state-byte (CPSB, currently 1174) with a per-transaction state-gas reservoir. Mostly affects transactions that SSTORE, CREATE, deploy proxies, or use EIP-7702 authorizations.
