Current Focus: EIP-8037

EIP-8037 introduces static cost-per-state-byte accounting for new state, with a per-transaction state-gas reservoir. This dashboard surfaces whether historical transactions still fit their original gas limits, how much extra outer gas they need when they do not, and whether runtime state gas spills beyond the reservoir.
