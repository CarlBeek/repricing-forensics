3. Reservoir Mechanics

By spec, the reservoir is currently dormant on mainnet. EIP-8037 sizes the reservoir as max(0, tx.gas − intrinsic_state_gas − TX_MAX_GAS_LIMIT); EIP-7825 caps tx.gas at 16.7M so schedule_initial_reservoir = 0 for every historical tx. State-gas charges currently come from gas_left at the new CPSB = 1530 rate. The reservoir mechanism activates once consensus relaxes the cap to let txs declare tx.gas > TX_MAX_GAS_LIMIT for state-only spend.

3a. Why these txs diverged

Divergent txs under EIP-8037 split into five mutually-exclusive buckets, prioritized in order so each tx lands in exactly one. runtime_state_gas and intrinsic_state_gas_only are the cohort actually exercising EIP-8037's headline mechanic; event_log_only + other_gas_accounting are tracking ancillary divergences the schedule induces (different refund timing, OOG cascades) without charging state gas; pre_execution_rejection is the EVM refusing the tx outright (e.g. adjusted gas_limit < baseline intrinsic).
