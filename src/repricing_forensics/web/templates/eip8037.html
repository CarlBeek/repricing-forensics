3. Reservoir Mechanics

EIP-8037 gives every transaction an initial state-gas reservoir, sized from intrinsic gas at transaction start. State operations (SSTORE, CREATE, new-account transfer, EIP-7702 authorization) draw from it. Once the reservoir is exhausted, the rest spills over and gets charged against regular gas. These three views show how full the reservoir gets in practice and what happens to the overflow tail.

3a. Utilization distribution

For every transaction, how full did its initial reservoir get? Most txs touch no state at all; a smaller cohort uses some; the overflow tail paid regular gas for the excess.
