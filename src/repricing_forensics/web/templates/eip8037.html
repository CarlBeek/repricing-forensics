EIP-8037: State Creation Repricing

Loading...

What EIP-8037 does. It charges state creation by the byte (CPSB = 1530 gas/byte) and segregates that charge into a second gas dimension so blocks are capped by max(regular_gas, state_gas). New accounts, new storage slots, EIP-7702 authorizations, and contract deployments all get materially more expensive. The reservoir mechanism that lets txs declare extra gas for state-only work is spec'd but currently dormant — EIP-7825's per-tx cap forces the reservoir to zero on every historical tx in this window.

Reservoir activation (dormant today)

By spec, the reservoir is sized as max(0, tx.gas − intrinsic_state_gas − TX_MAX_GAS_LIMIT); EIP-7825 caps tx.gas at 16.7M so schedule_initial_reservoir = 0 for every historical tx. State-gas charges currently come from gas_left at the new CPSB = 1530 rate. The reservoir mechanism activates once consensus relaxes the cap.
